## 1. The story in French and English

### 1

**Mon ami s’appelle Amine Al-Ghozzi, et c’est un poète extraordinaire.**
My friend’s name is Amine Al-Ghozzi, and he is an extraordinary poet.

* **s’appeler** = to be called
* **un poète** = a poet
* **extraordinaire** = extraordinary

**C’est un poète** literally means “It is a poet,” but here it means “He is a poet.”

---

### 2

**Il m’a donné un bout de papier, et il m’a dit :**
He gave me a piece of paper and said to me:

* **donner quelque chose à quelqu’un** = to give something to someone
* **dire quelque chose à quelqu’un** = to say/tell something to someone
* **un bout de papier** = a piece of paper

**m’** means “to me” or “me.”

---

### 3

**J’ai quelque chose pour toi.**
I have something for you.

* **j’ai** = I have
* **quelque chose** = something
* **pour toi** = for you

**toi** is the stressed form of “you,” used after a preposition:

* **pour toi** — for you
* **avec toi** — with you
* **sans toi** — without you

---

### 4

**C’est un poème que j’ai écrit.**
It is a poem that I wrote.

* **un poème** = a poem
* **écrire** = to write
* **j’ai écrit** = I wrote
* **que** = that/which

**Que** introduces information about the poem:

> I wrote the poem → a poem that I wrote

---

### 5

**J’aimerais que tu le chantes.**
I would like you to sing it.

* **j’aimerais** = I would like
* **tu** = you
* **le** = it, referring to **le poème**
* **chantes** = sing

The pattern is:

> **J’aimerais que tu + verb**
> I would like you to…

**Le** comes before the verb:

> **Tu chantes le poème.**
> You sing the poem.

> **Tu le chantes.**
> You sing it.

---

### 6

**J’ai grandi en Tunisie.**
I grew up in Tunisia.

* **grandir** = to grow up/grow
* **j’ai grandi** = I grew up
* **en Tunisie** = in Tunisia

**Grandir** emphasizes growing up. It is different from simply living somewhere:

* **J’ai grandi en Tunisie.** — I grew up in Tunisia.
* **J’ai vécu en Tunisie.** — I lived in Tunisia.

---

### 7

**Dans mon pays, en 2007, c’était la dictature.**
In my country, in 2007, it was a dictatorship.

* **dans** = in
* **mon pays** = my country
* **c’était** = it was
* **une dictature** = a dictatorship

**C’était** comes from:

> **ce + était**

It is the imperfect form of **être**.

---

### 8

**Ben Ali était devenu président après un coup d’État.**
Ben Ali had become president after a coup d’état.

* **devenir** = to become
* **était devenu** = had become
* **un coup d’État** = a coup d’état

**Était devenu** is the **plus-que-parfait**. It describes an earlier past event.

---

### 9

**Il était au pouvoir depuis 20 ans.**
He had been in power for 20 years.

* **être au pouvoir** = to be in power
* **depuis 20 ans** = for 20 years

French uses the imperfect here:

> **Il était au pouvoir depuis 20 ans.**

The English translation naturally uses “had been” because the duration is viewed from a past moment.

---

### 10

**Il y avait beaucoup de corruption, de répression et de pauvreté.**
There was a lot of corruption, repression, and poverty.

* **il y avait** = there was/were
* **beaucoup de** = a lot of/many
* **la corruption** = corruption
* **la répression** = repression
* **la pauvreté** = poverty

After **beaucoup**, use **de**:

> **beaucoup de pauvreté**
> a lot of poverty

---

### 11

**Le gouvernement pouvait mettre en prison tous les gens qui le critiquaient.**
The government could imprison everyone who criticized it.

* **le gouvernement** = the government
* **pouvoir** = to be able to/can
* **pouvait** = could
* **mettre quelqu’un en prison** = to imprison someone
* **tous les gens** = all the people/everyone
* **critiquer** = to criticize

In **qui le critiquaient**:

* **qui** = who, referring to the people
* **le** = it, referring to the government
* **critiquaient** = criticized

---

### 12

**Mais moi, je crois en la vérité.**
But I believe in the truth.

* **mais** = but
* **moi** = me/as for me
* **croire en** = to believe in
* **la vérité** = the truth

**Mais moi** adds emphasis:

> But I personally believe in the truth.

**Je crois** is present tense because it expresses a belief that remains true.

---

### 13

**C’est important de pouvoir dire ce qui ne va pas, et de parler d’un monde meilleur.**
It is important to be able to say what is wrong and to talk about a better world.

* **c’est important de + infinitive** = it is important to…
* **pouvoir dire** = to be able to say
* **ce qui ne va pas** = what is wrong
* **parler de** = to talk about
* **un monde meilleur** = a better world

**Ça va** means “it is okay.”

> **Ça ne va pas.**
> It is not okay / something is wrong.

**Meilleur** means “better”:

* **bon** — good
* **meilleur** — better
* **le meilleur** — the best

---

### 14

**Je trouvais le résultat formidable.**
I thought the result was wonderful.

* **trouver** = to find/think
* **je trouvais** = I thought
* **le résultat** = the result
* **formidable** = wonderful/terrific

The structure is:

> **trouver quelque chose + adjective**
> to find/consider something + adjective

Example:

> **Je trouve ce livre intéressant.**
> I find this book interesting.

---

### 15

**Mais je ne savais pas que cette chanson allait marquer mon pays, la Tunisie, à un moment important de son histoire.**
But I did not know that this song would leave its mark on my country, Tunisia, at an important moment in its history.

* **je ne savais pas** = I did not know
* **cette chanson** = this song
* **allait marquer** = was going to mark/would mark
* **mon pays** = my country
* **son histoire** = its history

**Allait + infinitive** is the future viewed from the past:

> **va marquer** — is going to mark
> **allait marquer** — was going to mark/would mark

**Marquer un pays** is often figurative:

> to leave a deep mark on a country

---

### 16

**C’était incroyable, mais notre chanson allait devenir l’hymne de la Révolution arabe.**
It was incredible, but our song would become the anthem of the Arab Revolution.

* **incroyable** = incredible
* **notre chanson** = our song
* **devenir** = to become
* **un hymne** = an anthem
* **la Révolution arabe** = the Arab Revolution

Again:

> **allait devenir** = was going to become/would become

---

### 17

**Bienvenue.**
Welcome.

This may be a transition or introduction to the next podcast section.

### 18

**J’aime beaucoup la musique tunisienne des années 1900 à 1950.**
I really like Tunisian music from 1900 to 1950.

* **j’aime beaucoup** = I really like
* **la musique tunisienne** = Tunisian music
* **des années 1900 à 1950** = from the years 1900 to 1950

The phrase **de… à…** means “from… to…”

---

### 19

**Les artistes étaient libres.**
The artists were free.

* **un artiste / une artiste** = an artist
* **étaient** = were
* **libre** = free

The imperfect describes the situation and background.

### 20

**Les voix des femmes étaient révolutionnaires, et les sujets de leurs chansons aussi : elles parlaient des cigarettes, du whisky et de leurs amants…**
Women’s voices were revolutionary, and so were the subjects of their songs: they talked about cigarettes, whisky, and their lovers…

* **une voix** = a voice
* **les voix** = voices
* **les femmes** = women
* **révolutionnaire** = revolutionary
* **un sujet** = a subject/topic
* **leurs chansons** = their songs
* **un amant** = a male lover

**Elles** refers to the women/artists.

* **parler de cigarettes** = talk about cigarettes
* **du whisky** = some/the whisky
  **de + le = du**

### 21

**Mais surtout, elles parlaient de leur liberté.**
But above all, they talked about their freedom.

* **surtout** = especially/above all
* **leur liberté** = their freedom

**Leur** is used before a singular noun:

> **leur liberté** — their freedom

Before a plural noun:

> **leurs chansons** — their songs

### 22

**Quand j’étais enfant, j’aimais découvrir tout ce qui était différent.**
When I was a child, I liked discovering everything that was different.

* **quand** = when
* **j’étais enfant** = I was a child
* **aimer + infinitive** = to like doing something
* **découvrir** = to discover
* **tout ce qui** = everything that
* **différent** = different

**Quand j’étais enfant** gives background information, so the imperfect is used.

I learned music all by myself.

* **apprendre** = to learn
* **j’ai appris** = I learned
* **tout seul/toute seule** = by oneself

* **les styles** = styles
* **j’aimais** = I liked

The imperfect suggests a repeated habit or continuing preference.

### 25

Inflation was very high.

* **l’inflation** = inflation
* **fort/forte** = strong/high

Because **inflation** is feminine:

> **forte**

Many people were unemployed.

* **beaucoup de gens** = many people

* **le chômage** = unemployment

* **les élites** = the elites

* **l’inégalité** = inequality
* **de plus en plus grande** = greater and greater

**De plus en plus** indicates gradual increase.

* **personne** = nobody/no one
* **avoir le droit de** = to have the right to
* **critiquer** = to criticize

> **Personne ne…**
> Nobody…

Before a vowel:

> **Personne n’avait…**

* **pauvre** = poor

# 2. Complete grammar summary

## A. Main verb tenses

### Present tense

Used for current facts, opinions, and general truths:

* **J’aime beaucoup…** — I really like…
* **Je crois en la vérité.** — I believe in the truth.
* **C’est important…** — It is important.

### Passé composé

Used for completed events:

* **J’ai grandi.** — I grew up.
* **Il m’a donné…** — He gave me…
* **J’ai écrit…** — I wrote…
* **J’ai appris…** — I learned…
* **J’ai chanté…** — I sang…
* **Ils m’ont appelée.** — They called me.

Structure:

> **avoir/être + past participle**

Examples:

* **j’ai donné**
* **j’ai dit**
* **j’ai écrit**
* **j’ai appris**
* **j’ai chanté**
* **ils ont appelé**

### Imparfait

Used for:

* background descriptions
* repeated habits
* states and conditions
* continuing situations

Examples:

* **c’était la dictature**
* **les artistes étaient libres**
* **j’étais enfant**
* **j’aimais découvrir**
* **l’inflation était forte**
* **le gouvernement contrôlait**
* **personne n’avait le droit**

### Plus-que-parfait

Used for something that had already happened before another past event:

> **Ben Ali était devenu président.**
> Ben Ali had become president.

Structure:

> imperfect of **avoir/être** + past participle

### Future in the past

Structure:

> **imperfect of aller + infinitive**

Examples:

* **allait marquer** — would mark
* **allait devenir** — would become

---

## B. Relative pronouns

### Qui

**Qui** is the subject of the following verb:

> **Les gens qui le critiquaient**
> The people who criticized it

The people perform the action.

> **La femme qui chante**
> The woman who sings

### Que

**Que** is the object of the following verb:

> **Un poème que j’ai écrit**
> A poem that I wrote

I wrote the poem. The poem receives the action.

### Ce qui

> **ce qui ne va pas**
> what is wrong

Examples:

* **Je sais ce qui ne va pas.** — I know what is wrong.
* **Tout ce qui est nouveau** — everything that is new.

---

## C. Object pronouns

### Me / m’

> **Il m’a donné un papier.**
> He gave me a paper.

> **Ils m’ont appelée.**
> They called me.

**Me** becomes **m’** before a vowel:

* **m’a**
* **m’ont**

### Le

> **Tu le chantes.**
> You sing it.

**Le** replaces a masculine noun such as **un poème**.

### Pronoun position

Object pronouns normally come before the conjugated verb:

* **Je chante le poème.**

* **Je le chante.**

* **Ils appellent Emel.**

* **Ils m’appellent.**

---

## D. Negation

### Ne… pas

> **Je ne savais pas.**
> I did not know.

### Personne ne…

> **Personne n’avait le droit…**
> Nobody had the right…

The word **personne** means “nobody” and normally appears with **ne**.

---

## E. Possessive adjectives

| Owner  | Singular noun     | Plural noun        |
| ------ | ----------------- | ------------------ |
| I      | **mon pays**      | **mes chansons**   |
| you    | **ton pays**      | **tes chansons**   |
| he/she | **son histoire**  | **ses illusions**  |
| we     | **notre chanson** | **nos styles**     |
| they   | **leur liberté**  | **leurs chansons** |

The possessive agrees with the thing owned, not the owner.

* **son histoire** because **histoire** is singular
* **leurs chansons** because **chansons** is plural

## F. Articles and contractions

### À + le = au

> **au pouvoir**

### De + le = du

> **du whisky**

### De + un = d’un

> **d’un monde meilleur**

### De + les = des

> **des cigarettes**

### De + vowel = d’

> **d’un**
> **décider des salaires**
> **parler d’un monde**

### Aimer + infinitive

> **J’aime chanter.**

> **J’aimais découvrir.**

> **C’est important de parler.**

> **avoir le droit de critiquer**
> to have the right to criticize

### Pouvoir + infinitive

> **pouvoir dire**
> to be able to say

### Parler de + noun

> **parler de musique**
> to talk about music

### Mettre quelqu’un en prison

> to imprison someone

### Être au chômage

> to be unemployed

> to be in power

## H. Adjective agreement

Adjectives agree with the noun:

* **un monde meilleur**
* **une musique tunisienne**
* **une inflation forte**
* **une chanson révolutionnaire**
* **toute seule** — female
* **tout seul** — male

Some adjectives have the same masculine and feminine form:

* **extraordinaire**
* **révolutionnaire**
* **incroyable**
* **formidable**

### De plus en plus

> **de plus en plus grande**

### Meilleur

* **bon** — good
* **meilleur** — better

# 5. Pronunciation and listening notes

* **mon ami** → the words link together: *mon-na-mi*
* **il m’a** → sounds like one unit: *eel-ma*
* **j’ai écrit** → *zhay-ay-kree*
* **c’était** → *say-tay*
* **pouvoir** → *poo-vwar*
* **depuis** → *duh-pwee*
* **beaucoup de** → *bo-koo duh*
* **chantes** and **chante** sound identical
* The final **s** in **chantes** is silent.
* The final **t** in **bout** is silent.
* The final **x** in **voix** is silent.
* French often links words in speech, especially before a vowel.
