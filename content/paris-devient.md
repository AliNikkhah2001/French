# Overview

The refrain describes Paris becoming intimate and small in another person’s arms. Use this lesson to learn **devenir**, useful negation, and concrete Paris vocabulary.

# Transcript

| French | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Dans tes bras, Paris devient une petite chambre. | In your arms, Paris becomes a small room. | `devenir` = become. |
| Une île en plein jeudi où le temps se déshabille. | An island in the middle of Thursday where time undresses. | Highly poetic. |
| Paris pose sa couronne fragile. | Paris sets down its fragile crown. | Personification. |
| Je n’ai plus de pays que ton souffle sur ma ville. | I no longer have a homeland except your breath over my city. | Poetic `ne...plus` and `ne...que`. |
| Les pavés s’inclinent. | The cobblestones bow. | `s’incliner` = bow/lean. |
| Un bateau s’éloigne. | A boat moves away. | `s’éloigner`. |
| Si tu t’en allais, que resterait-il ? | If you went away, what would remain? | Hypothetical imperfect + conditional. |

# Grammar

## Devenir

`Devenir` means **to become**.

- je deviens
- tu deviens
- il/elle devient
- nous devenons
- vous devenez
- ils/elles deviennent

## Ne... plus

`Ne... plus` means **no longer / not anymore**.

- `Je ne travaille plus ici.` — I no longer work here.

## Ne... que

`Ne... que` means **only**.

- `Je n’ai que cinq minutes.` — I only have five minutes.

# Collocations

| French | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| devenir une chambre | to become a room | Paris devient une chambre. |
| poser sa couronne | to set down one’s crown | La ville pose sa couronne. |
| ne plus avoir | to no longer have | Je n’ai plus de temps. |
| s’éloigner lentement | to move away slowly | Le bateau s’éloigne lentement. |

# Important notes

- The imagery is deliberately literary; prioritize the reusable grammar over literal imitation.
- `ne...plus` and `ne...que` are both common and useful outside poetry.
