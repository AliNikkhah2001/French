# Overview

The automatic transcript repeatedly distorted **Montmartre** into forms such as `mon murre` and `meurtre`. This lesson keeps only clear, useful lines and focuses on conversational structures inside the poetic setting.

# Grammar

## On = we

In everyday French, `on` very often replaces `nous`.

- `On ne sait rien.` — We know nothing.
- `On la suivra.` — We will follow it.

## S’enfuir

`S’enfuir` means to run away or flee.

- je m’enfuis
- tu t’enfuis
- il/elle s’enfuit
- nous nous enfuyons

## Si + présent → futur

Use this for a realistic future possibility.

- `Si la nuit s’enfuit, on la suivra.`

# Important notes

- `Montmartre` is a place name in Paris; do not learn the transcript’s ASR variants.
- `Tes yeux boivent mes mots` is poetic, not ordinary everyday phrasing.
- `Tant pis` is worth learning as a whole expression.
