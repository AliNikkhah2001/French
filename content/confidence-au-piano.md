# Grammar

## Comme si + imparfait

Use `comme si` with the imperfect to compare reality with an imagined situation.

- `Comme si demain n’était plus rien.` — As if tomorrow no longer mattered.
- `Il parle comme s’il savait tout.` — He talks as if he knew everything.

## Se lancer

`Se lancer` literally means to launch oneself, but in everyday French it often means **to go for it** or **to take the plunge**.

- `J’hésite, puis je me lance.` — I hesitate, then I go for it.

## Pour ne pas avoir à + infinitif

This structure means **so as not to have to...**

- `Je pars tôt pour ne pas avoir à attendre.` — I leave early so I do not have to wait.
