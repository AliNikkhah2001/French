# Overview

This lesson is built from clear sections of the supplied transcript and focuses on remembering someone who has left. It is especially useful for `rattraper`, realistic future conditions, and the contrast between present memories and future possibilities.

# Grammar

## Voir + infinitif

French can use `voir + infinitif` to say that someone witnesses an action.

- `Je t’ai vu partir.` — I saw you leave.

## Si + présent → futur

Use this pattern for a realistic future possibility.

- `Si tu reviens, je laisserai la porte ouverte.`

Compare with a more hypothetical form:

- `Si tu revenais, je laisserais la porte ouverte.`

## Encore

Depending on context, `encore` can mean **still**, **again**, or **more**.

- `Il danse encore.` — He is still dancing / He dances again.
