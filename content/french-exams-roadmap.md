# Overview

French qualifications fall into two broad families:

- **Diplomas** such as DELF and DALF certify a specific CEFR level.
- **Tests** such as TCF and TEF assess your level for a particular purpose.

For a learner with about one month of study, **A1 communication** is the immediate goal. DELF A1 is the first natural exam target if you want a diploma. TCF Canada or TEF Canada only becomes relevant when you have a real Canadian pathway and know its current language requirements.

# Exam guide

## CEFR levels

| Level | Practical ability | Common qualification |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | introductions and very basic everyday needs | DELF A1 |
| A2 | routine exchanges and simple descriptions | DELF A2 |
| B1 | experiences, opinions, and plans | DELF B1 |
| B2 | defend ideas with reasonable fluency | DELF B2 |
| C1 | flexible use of complex French | DALF C1 |
| C2 | highly precise and nuanced mastery | DALF C2 |

## DELF and DALF

DELF consists of independent diplomas at A1, A2, B1, and B2. DALF continues at C1 and C2. These qualifications assess listening, reading, writing, and speaking.

- [Official DELF information](https://www.france-education-international.fr/en/diplome/delf-tout-public)
- [Official DALF information](https://www.france-education-international.fr/en/diplome/dalf)

## TCF

TCF is a level test with different versions for different purposes. TCF tout public assesses general French. TCF Canada is designed for Canadian immigration and citizenship procedures.

- [TCF tout public](https://www.france-education-international.fr/en/test/tcf-tout-public)
- [TCF Canada](https://www.france-education-international.fr/en/test/tcf-canada)
- [Official sample questions](https://www.france-education-international.fr/en/test/exemples-epreuves-tcf)

## TEF Canada

The official provider currently describes four skills for a Canadian immigration application: reading, listening, writing, and speaking. Test validity, required scores, and immigration thresholds can change.

- [TEF Canada overview](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/en/candidate/test-evaluation-francais/tef-canada/presentation/)
- [TEF Canada preparation](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/en/candidate/test-evaluation-francais/tef-canada/preparation/)

## Choosing an exam

| Goal | Likely starting point |
|---|---|
| permanent beginner diploma | DELF A1 or A2 |
| permanent intermediate diploma | DELF B1 or B2 |
| advanced diploma | DALF C1 or C2 |
| general level assessment | TCF tout public |
| Canadian immigration or citizenship | TCF Canada or TEF Canada, according to current program rules |
| university admission | the exact test and score accepted by the institution |

Always verify the current rules with the receiving authority before paying or registering.

# Important notes

- Exam formats, prices, registration dates, score conversions, and immigration thresholds can change.
- “French equivalent of IELTS” is not a one-to-one concept. IELTS tests English; French qualifications serve different purposes.
- Do not spend your first months memorizing exam tricks. Build listening, vocabulary, grammar, speaking, and writing first.
