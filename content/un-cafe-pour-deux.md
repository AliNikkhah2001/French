# Overview

**Un Café Pour Deux** is one of the clearest songs in the supplied transcript for A2 learners. The imagery is poetic, but the grammar and café vocabulary are highly reusable.

# Transcript

| French | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Deux cuillères dorment côte à côte. | Two spoons sleep side by side. | The transcript misheard `Deux`. |
| Un café pour deux et le monde un peu moins lourd. | A coffee for two and the world a little less heavy. | `un peu moins` = a little less. |
| Tes doigts tremblent dans les miens. | Your fingers tremble in mine. | `les miens` replaces `mes doigts`. |
| Tu parles bas de tes dimanches. | You speak softly about your Sundays. | `parler bas` = speak quietly. |
| Si tu restais jusqu’à la pluie... | If you stayed until the rain... | Hypothetical `si + imparfait`. |
| Si tu t’en vas, laisse au moins un peu de toi. | If you leave, at least leave a little of yourself. | `s’en aller` = leave. |
| Quand tu veux, je bois tes yeux. | Whenever you want, I drink in your eyes. | Poetic metaphor. |

# Grammar

## Possessive pronouns

- `mes doigts` → `les miens`
- `ma main` → `la mienne`

The possessive pronoun agrees with the noun being replaced.

## S’en aller

`S’en aller` means to leave or go away.

- `je m’en vais`
- `tu t’en vas`
- `il/elle s’en va`
- `nous nous en allons`

## Si + imparfait

`Si tu restais...` introduces a hypothetical situation.

# Important notes

- `Deux cuillères` is the plausible correction of the transcript’s `De cuillères`.
- `Je bois tes yeux` is poetic, not literal everyday language.
