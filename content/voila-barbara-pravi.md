# Overview

This lesson studies **direct address**, the present tense, and the presentative **voilà**.

Listen first on the [official Eurovision performance](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-9t_SwPN31s). Full lyrics are not reproduced here; use the official recording or a licensed lyrics view, then practice the study lines below.

- Say who is present: `me voilà`.
- Use the present tense for a live confession.
- Notice short stage vocabulary: `la scène`, `la lumière`, `le silence`.

# Grammar

## Voilà and voici

Use **voilà** for something farther or newly revealed, and **voici** for something near.

- `Me voilà.` — Here I am.
- `Voici ma chanson.` — Here is my song.

## Depuis + present

Use the present after **depuis** for an action that started earlier and continues now.

- `Je chante depuis dix ans.` — I have sung for ten years.
- `Elle attend depuis ce matin.` — She has waited since this morning.

## Direct address with tu and vous

Use **tu** for one familiar person and **vous** for formality, respect, or several people.

- `Tu écoutes bien ?` — Are you listening?
- `Vous êtes prêts ?` — Are you ready?
