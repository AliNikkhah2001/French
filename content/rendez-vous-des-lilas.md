# Overview

This lesson uses the clearest parts of the supplied transcript of **Rendez-vous des lilas**. It is especially useful for café vocabulary and idiomatic verb patterns.

# Grammar

## Faire semblant de + infinitif

Means **to pretend to...**

- `Je fais semblant de comprendre.` — I pretend to understand.
- `Il fait semblant de dormir.` — He pretends to sleep.

## Tant que

`Tant que` means **as long as** while a condition continues.

- `Tant que tu es là, je reste.` — As long as you are here, I stay.

## Possessive pronoun: la mienne

`La mienne` means **mine** and replaces a feminine singular noun.

- `ta joue cherche la mienne` — your cheek seeks mine

# Important notes

- `Rendez-vous` is unchanged in the plural: `des rendez-vous`.
- `Ton manteau boit la lumière` is literary imagery; the grammar is simple, but the phrase is not ordinary literal speech.
