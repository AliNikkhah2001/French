# Overview

The transcript repeatedly misheard **Lettre Jamais Envoyée** as `l’être jamais envoyé`. This lesson restores the meaningful title and uses clear lines about writing, memory, and things left unsaid.

# Grammar

## Ne... jamais

`Jamais` means never when used with negation.

- `Je ne l’ai jamais envoyée.` — I never sent it.

In titles and noun phrases, `ne` may be absent: `Lettre jamais envoyée`.

## Oser + infinitif

`Oser` means to dare.

- `J’ose parler.` — I dare to speak.
- `Si j’osais parler...` — If I dared to speak...

## Conditional questions

Formal or literary questions may use inversion:

- `Changerais-tu...?` — Would you change...?

# Important notes

- `lettre` and `l’être` can sound close in noisy audio, but they are unrelated words.
- The title is best understood as a noun phrase: **a letter never sent**.
