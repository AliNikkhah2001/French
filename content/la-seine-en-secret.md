# Grammar

## Relative clauses with qui

`Qui` can replace the subject of a relative clause.

- `les ponts qui veillent` — the bridges that keep watch
- `les couples qui passent` — the couples who pass by

## Se souvenir de

`Se souvenir de` means **to remember**.

- `Je me souviens de Paris.` — I remember Paris.
- `La nuit se souvient de nos baisers.` — The night remembers our kisses.

## Si + imparfait → conditionnel présent

Use this pattern for a hypothetical present or future situation.

- `Si tu revenais, je laisserais...`
- `Si j’avais le temps, je voyagerais.`

# Important notes

- `La Seine` is a proper noun; `la scène` means stage/scene. They sound similar but are different words.
- The automatic transcript contains severe recognition errors around `péniches`; do not memorize those raw captions.
- The imagery is poetic, but `se souvenir de`, `s’attarder`, and `si + imparfait → conditionnel` are highly reusable.
