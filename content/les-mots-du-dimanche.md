# Overview

This song is useful for phrases about hesitation, saying too little, and gathering courage. The supplied transcript is noisy, so this lesson keeps only clear, pedagogically useful material.

# Grammar

## De peur de + infinitif

Means **for fear of doing...**

- `Elle se tait de peur de déranger.` — She stays quiet for fear of disturbing anyone.

## Au lieu de + infinitif

Means **instead of doing...**

- `Au lieu de parler, écoute.` — Instead of talking, listen.

## Direct object pronouns

`les` can replace a plural noun already known.

- `Je vois les mots.` → `Je les vois.`
- `Je les écoute.` — I listen to them.
