This lesson studies **promises**, the future tense, and direct speech.

# Grammar

## Promettre with an indirect object

**Promettre** often takes the person receiving the promise as an indirect object.

- `Je te promets.` — I promise you.
- `Elle me promet une lettre.` — She promises me a letter.

## Futur simple

Use the future for promises and plans.

- `Je reviendrai.` — I will come back.
- `Nous partirons.` — We will leave.
- `Je dirai.` — I will say.

## Ne ... jamais

Place the two parts around the conjugated verb.

- `Je ne dis jamais adieu.` — I never say goodbye.
- `Il ne part jamais seul.` — He never leaves alone.
