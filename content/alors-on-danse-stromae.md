# Grammar

## Alors as consequence

**Alors** often introduces a result after a reason.

- `Je suis fatigué, alors je danse.` — I am tired, so I dance.
- `Il pleut, alors nous restons.` — It is raining, so we stay.

## Reflexive verbs

Many daily feelings and routines use reflexive pronouns.

- `Je me lève.` — I get up.
- `Elle se repose.` — She rests.
- `Nous nous amusons.` — We have fun.

## Malgré + noun

Use **malgré** for “despite” or “in spite of.”

- `Malgré la fatigue, il danse.` — Despite fatigue, he dances.
- `Malgré le bruit, elle dort.` — Despite the noise, she sleeps.
