# Overview

This lesson studies **questions**, absence, and compressed spoken French around the title’s wordplay, **“papa, où t’es ?”**

Watch the [official music video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oiKj0Z_Xnjc). Full lyrics are not reproduced here; use the official recording or a licensed lyric view, then practice the study lines below.

- Learn **où** for place and **ou** for choice.
- Practice questions with inversion and question words.
- Use short present-tense sentences about family and searching.

# Grammar

## Où versus ou

**Où** asks “where”; **ou** means “or.”

- `Où est ton père ?` — Where is your father?
- `Toi ou moi ?` — You or me?

## Asking questions

French questions can use a question word, inversion, or rising intonation.

- `Où est ton père ?` — Where is your father?
- `Cherches-tu ton père ?` — Are you looking for your father?
- `Tu cherches ton père ?` — You’re looking for your father?

## Imparfait for repeated past searching

Use the imperfect for repeated or background past actions.

- `Il cherchait son père partout.` — He used to look for his father everywhere.
- `Elle posait toujours la même question.` — She always asked the same question.

# Important notes

- Full lyrics are intentionally omitted. Practice with the official video and a licensed lyric source.
- **Papaoutai** compresses **“papa, où t’es ?”**; in careful speech, say **“papa, où es-tu ?”**
- Do not confuse **où** (“where”) with **ou** (“or”).
