# Overview

This lesson focuses on memory, return, and the very useful French prefix `re-`, which often carries the idea of doing something again.

# Grammar

## The prefix re-

French often uses `re-` to express repetition or return.

- `venir` → `revenir` — to come back
- `partir` → `repartir` — to leave again
- `voir` → `revoir` — to see again

## Future tense

`Restera` means **will remain**.

Common future endings are `-ai, -as, -a, -ons, -ez, -ont`.

- `Je resterai.` — I will stay.
- `Il restera.` — He will stay.

# Important notes

- `Un souvenir` can mean a memory or a souvenir/keepsake.
- The prefix `re-` is a useful clue but does not guarantee a verb’s full meaning; learn each common verb as a unit.
